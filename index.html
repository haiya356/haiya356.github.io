A Little About Me

Kevs Caffein is a cafe that focuses on serving fresh and delicious drinks. I specialize in Thai tea and matcha, offering a variety of flavors for everyone to enjoy. Whether you need a drink to start your day or something refreshing during the afternoon, I have something for you. I want Kevs Caffein to be a comfortable place where people can relax, enjoy their drinks, and spend time with others. My goal is to make every visit a good experience.

Quality Ingredients

I Believe that great drinks start with great ingredients. This is exactly why I focus on using quality Thai Tea and matcha powders to bring out its greatest flavors in every drink
